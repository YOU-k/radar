# CVD in China/East Asia and Chinese/East Asian cohort and omics resources (for a ChinaHEART analyst), as of Sept 2026

Scope note: web research done 2026-09-27 plus the local evidence base `/data/yy_data/radar/background/cardio-omics/report.md` (cited below as "local report [n]", with DOIs). Several publisher pages (Lancet, JACC, ckbiobank.org) returned HTTP 403, so some numbers come from secondary summaries (tctmd, search-engine abstracts). Those are marked. Anything I could not check this session is in the Gaps sections, not stated as fact.

## Q1. Epidemiological differences vs Western populations

### Takeaway
China's CVD burden is larger and more stroke-heavy than in Western countries. Intracerebral haemorrhage (ICH) makes up roughly a third of community strokes versus about 12% in white populations, and intracranial atherosclerosis is common. Hypertension is very common and very poorly controlled: 7.2% control among 35–75-year-olds in the China PEACE MPP. In 2023 high SBP and ambient PM pollution were the top two attributable CVD risk factors.

### Cited Findings
- **Burden (2023):** 11.1 million incident CVD cases, 135.8 million prevalent cases and 4.5 million CVD deaths in China in 2023 (GBD analysis) — [Trends in CVD burden in China 1990–2023, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13491092/)
- CVD caused 48.98% of rural and 47.35% of urban deaths, about 2 in 5 deaths; the Chinese CVD report estimates ~330 million people with CVD — [Report on Cardiovascular Health and Diseases in China 2023, BES](https://www.besjournal.com/en/article/doi/10.3967/bes2024.162); also [2024 annual report, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12700738/)
- Stroke incidence was 491.0/100,000 in 2023 (national surveillance). GBD counted 4.09 million new strokes in China in 2021, up 142.6% from 1990 — [JACC 2025 national surveillance incidence estimates](https://www.jacc.org/doi/10.1016/j.jacc.2025.08.060) (figures from a search abstract; the full text returned 403)
- In 2023 the five leading CVD risk factors in China were **high SBP, ambient particulate matter pollution, high LDL-C, smoking, lead exposure** — [GBD China 1990–2023, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13491092/). This supports the air-pollution point: ambient PM ranks second, ahead of LDL-C.
- **Stroke subtypes:** in a systematic review of community-based studies, ICH was 33% of strokes in Chinese populations versus 12% in white populations. The review found slightly higher overall stroke incidence in Chinese populations — [Tsai et al., Neurology 2013](https://www.neurology.org/doi/10.1212/WNL.0b013e31829bfde3)
- ICH makes up 18–47% of strokes in China (as high as 55.4% in Changsha) versus about 10–15% in the West — [Liu et al., "Stroke and Stroke Care in China", Stroke](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/strokeaha.111.635755)
- **Intracranial atherosclerosis (ICAS):** intracranial stenosis prevalence was 46.6% in CICAS (ischaemic stroke/TIA patients). About 44.3% of acute intracranial artery occlusions in a Chinese series were atherosclerotic, higher than in Western series — search summary of [Stroke in China: advances and challenges (review PDF)](https://file.medtrib.cn/pdf/8a9d9fa0-a7c7-4ce5-8ade-30478b5b3a3c/Stroke%20in%20China_%20advances%20and%20challenges%20in%20epidemiology_%20prevention_%20and%20management.pdf) and [sICAS burden paper, PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6284755/)
- **Hypertension (China PEACE MPP, Lancet 2017; Lu J et al., Lancet 390:2549–58):** 1.74 million adults aged 35–75 from all 31 provinces, enrolled Sept 2014 – June 2017. Crude prevalence 44.7% (37.2% age/sex-standardised), awareness 44.7%, treatment 30.1%, control 7.2%. Control was below 30% in every one of 264,475 subgroups. 8.1% of primary-care sites stocked no antihypertensives, and only 33.8% stocked all four major classes — [tctmd summary](https://www.tctmd.com/news/hypertension-sweeps-china-awareness-treatment-and-control-remain-poor); citation confirmed at [Lancet abstract page](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(17)32478-9/abstract) / [PubMed 29102084](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29102084)
- Primary-care institutional characteristics linked to hypertension awareness, treatment and control were analysed in PEACE MPP together with a primary-care survey — [Lancet Global Health 2022](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/langlo/article/PIIS2214-109X(22)00428-4/fulltext)
- **Salt:** SSaSS enrolled about 21,000 rural Chinese adults with prior stroke, or aged ≥60 with uncontrolled hypertension. A salt substitute (75% NaCl / 25% KCl) cut stroke from 33.65 to 29.14 per 1,000 person-years (P=0.006), roughly a 14% reduction in stroke, 13% in MACE and 12% in death over about 5 years, with no excess of hyperkalaemia serious adverse events (NEJM 2021) — [ACC trial summary](https://www.acc.org/Latest-in-Cardiology/Clinical-Trials/2021/08/26/01/19/SSaSS); [NCT02092090](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02092090)
- A secondary SSaSS analysis split the BP reduction into sodium-reduction and potassium-increase components — [PMC11001572](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11001572/)
- **Air pollution, ChinaHEART-specific:** a ChinaHEART analysis links clean-air policy to less atherogenic lipid profiles — [J Hazardous Materials 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304389424019733)
- **Prevention gap:** in ChinaHEART fewer than 3% of high-risk people received statins or aspirin, only 34.2% of people with prior stroke/heart disease used preventive drugs, and only 2.8% had all four healthy lifestyles. The prevalence of high CVD risk varied about 14-fold across regions — [ChinaHEART cohort profile, IJE 2023](https://academic.oup.com/ije/article/52/5/e273/7187439)

### Inferences
- A ChinaHEART outcome model that follows Western practice by pooling "ASCVD" (dominated by MI) will be driven mostly by stroke. Haemorrhagic stroke has a different risk-factor profile: steeper BP dependence and an inverse or flat LDL-C relation. Splitting stroke subtypes is therefore essential. Mortality linkage alone mostly cannot separate them (see Q3 gaps).
- The 2014–2022 enrolment window spans large changes: the clean-air action plan (2013+), expanding hypertension/statin programmes and COVID. Calendar-period and region effects are likely to confound time-trend and exposure analyses.

### Gaps
- I could not fetch the full JACC 2025 surveillance paper, so its stroke subtype split (ischaemic vs ICH vs SAH) for 2023 is unverified here.
- I found no verified 2024–2026 update on the ICH share. The ~33% figure comes from older community studies (pre-2013 review). The ICH share is widely reported to have fallen as BP control improved, but I did not verify that this session.
- Sodium intake numbers (e.g., "~10–11 g salt/day" in China) were not verified this session.

## Q2. Genetic differences relevant to drug targets and PRS

### Takeaway
East Asians carry functional variants that are nearly absent in Europeans, notably ALDH2 rs671 and ADH1B rs1229984. These make China uniquely suited to alcohol MR. CKB MR with them showed that alcohol causally raises stroke risk (ICH especially), with no protective effect on MI. East Asian pQTL maps differ substantially from European ones. CAD PRS built mainly on European data add little discrimination in Chinese populations.

### Cited Findings
- **ALDH2/ADH1B MR (CKB, Lancet 2019, Millwood et al.):** 512,715 adults were followed about 10 years. 161,498 were genotyped for ALDH2-rs671 and ADH1B-rs1229984. The rs671 alcohol-decreasing allele is essentially not found in European populations. Genotype-predicted alcohol intake was **not** associated with MI but **was** associated with higher stroke risk. This argues against the J-shaped "moderate drinking protects" hypothesis for stroke — [Lancet 2019](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(18)31772-0/fulltext); [PubMed 30955975](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30955975/) (full text 403; findings from abstract/search snippet)
- The rs671 A allele slows conversion of acetaldehyde to acetate. The rs1229984 T allele speeds conversion of ethanol to acetaldehyde — same source as above (search summary)
- A related CKB MR in Chinese men (AJCN 2022) looked at alcohol and CVD / all-cause mortality — [AJCN 2022](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0002916522000910)
- rs671 is associated with clustering of CV risk factors in a large Chinese dataset, with associations that differ by sex — [PMC7716427](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7716427/)
- A Taiwan Biobank MR looked at moderate alcohol and CV risk factors — [PMC11480056](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11480056/)
- **PCSK9 variant spectrum differs by ancestry.** R46L has MAF 1.6% in Caucasians but 0.28% in African Americans. A443T has MAF 9.4% in African Americans but 0.048% in Caucasians. A rare loss-of-function variant, C378W, is described in Taiwanese individuals — [PMC3661383](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3661383/); [PMC12470178](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12470178/)
- In UKB (n=408,039), LPA and PCSK9 LoF carriers had lower CAD risk (OR 0.91 and 0.81), and carriers of both had OR 0.73 — [Nat Cardiovasc Res 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44161-026-00865-9)
- A PheWAS of a Lp-PLA2 (PLA2G7) loss-of-function variant was run in 90,000 Chinese adults. The variant (V279F) is East Asian-specific and was used as a natural experiment for the darapladib target — [PMC5100610](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5100610/)
- **CAD GWAS (Japan):** BBJ ran a GWAS of 168,228 Japanese (25,892 cases), found 8 new loci plus Japanese-specific rare variants tied to severity and CV mortality. A trans-ancestry meta-analysis of >600,000 people (with CARDIoGRAMplusC4D and UKB) found 35 more new loci, including HMGCR (Koyama et al., Nat Genet 2020) — [Nat Genet 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-020-0705-3); [AMED release](https://www.amed.go.jp/en/news/release_20201006-02.html)
- **Stroke GWAS:** GIGASTROKE (Nature 2022) combined >200,000 stroke cases and >2 million controls across 5 ancestries, including BBJ and CKB. It found 89 loci (61 new). FGF5 variants were particularly associated with ischaemic stroke and BP traits in East Asians — [PubMed 36180795](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36180795/); [CKB news](https://www.ckbiobank.org/news-1/stroke-genetics-informs-drug-discovery-and-risk-prediction-across-ancestries)
- A proteome-wide MR in East Asians and Europeans found ischaemic stroke therapeutic targets (Stroke 2025) — [Stroke](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/STROKEAHA.125.050982)
- **PRS transferability:** in about 100,000 genotyped CKB participants, 10 published CAD PRS were tested and new ones built (C+T, LDpred). The best PRS added only modest discrimination, roughly ΔC ≈ 0.01 and NRI 3.5%, which the authors blamed on the lack of large Chinese CAD GWAS (Chin Med J 2023) — local report [11], https://doi.org/10.1097/CM9.0000000000002694
- A contrasting, more positive result came from the China-PAR-based prospective Chinese cohort: a CAD PRS "improves risk stratification" (Lu X et al., EHJ 2022) — local report [38], https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehac093
- Population-specific PRS for Han Chinese ancestry have been built from TPMI — [PMC12675292](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12675292/)

### Inferences
- For ChinaHEART, rs671 genotype (if biospecimens can be genotyped) is a strong instrument for alcohol in stroke-subtype, BP and AF analyses. Its effect runs mostly through men, because Chinese women drink little. Pleiotropy via acetaldehyde and the sex asymmetry are known issues.
- Target conclusions drawn from European MR (e.g., the effect size of Lp(a) or PCSK9 on stroke subtypes) may not transfer directly, because the Chinese outcome mix is stroke-heavy and allele frequencies differ.

### Gaps (not verified this session; do not state as fact without checking)
- Frequency of rs671 A allele (commonly cited ~15–30% in Chinese; about 30–40% carriers) — not fetched.
- The quantitative Lp(a) distribution in Chinese vs European populations (Chinese levels are usually described as lower on average, with smaller apo(a) isoform differences) — no primary source retrieved.
- ANGPTL3 LoF frequencies in East Asians — no source retrieved.
- Exact MR effect sizes in the Lancet 2019 CKB paper (e.g., the per-280 g/week stroke RR) — full text blocked (403).

## Q3. What ChinaHEART / China PEACE Million Persons Project is

### Takeaway
ChinaHEART (formerly China PEACE MPP) is a government-funded national CVD screening and management programme, run from Fuwai Hospital/NCCD. From 2014 to 2022 it enrolled 4.40 million adults aged 35–75 at 353 sites in all 31 provinces and identified about 1 million high-risk participants. It is very large but relatively thin per person. Point-of-care lipids and glucose come from everyone, and ECG, carotid ultrasound and echo only from the high-risk/control subsample. It holds stored blood and urine from about 2.7 million people. Follow-up is mainly death-registry linkage (median 3.6 years at profile), plus active follow-up of high-risk participants. Data access is limited to researchers in China.

### Cited Findings (IJE 2023 cohort profile unless stated)
- Name: China Health Evaluation And risk Reduction through nationwide Teamwork; formerly China PEACE Million Persons Project — [IJE 2023 cohort profile](https://academic.oup.com/ije/article/52/5/e273/7187439); [ScienceDirect paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666667724000035)
- Funding came from China's Ministry of Finance and National Health Commission, plus the CAMS Innovation Fund. Senior investigators are Shengshou Hu and Xi Li (Fuwai/NCCD) — [IJE 2023](https://academic.oup.com/ije/article/52/5/e273/7187439)
- **Enrolment:** 4,404,586 participants in 2014–2022, out of about 11.4 million invited (>35% response). They came from 353 sites (141 urban districts, 212 rural counties) across all 31 provinces. Eligibility was age 35–75 and ≥6 months' residence. Recruitment used local publicity campaigns, so this is a convenience or volunteer sample, not a probability sample — [IJE 2023](https://academic.oup.com/ije/article/52/5/e273/7187439)
- Other papers give different site counts for specific phases, e.g., 286 sites (170 rural, 116 urban) in 2015–2021 — per search snippet on [Chin Med J LDL-C paper](https://www.ovid.com/jnls/cmj/fulltext/10.1097/cm9.0000000000003199~association-and-its-population-heterogeneities-between). Always check which analytic subset a paper uses.
- **High-risk definition:** any one of (i) prior major CVD event; (ii) WHO/ISH 10-year risk ≥20%; (iii) LDL-C ≥4.14 or HDL-C <0.78 mmol/L; (iv) SBP >160 or DBP >100 mmHg. 998,673 participants were high-risk.
- **Baseline data:**
  - Questionnaire: demographics, SES, smoking, alcohol, disease history, medications, family history, EQ-5D.
  - Exam: BP twice, heart rate, height, weight.
  - Labs: point-of-care TC, TG, HDL-C, LDL-C and glucose; urine dipstick.
  - High-risk plus control subsample: 12-lead ECG, carotid ultrasound, echocardiography, detailed diet, physical activity and reproductive history.
  - Biospecimens: blood and urine stored from about 2.7 million participants.
- **Follow-up:** passive linkage to the National Mortality Surveillance System and vital registration (ICD-10 causes), with 16.3 million person-years and median 3.6 years at the time of the profile. High-risk participants have active face-to-face follow-up (annual for very high risk, biennial otherwise, with telephone contact in alternate years), with about 85% attendance. A resurvey of 82,403 non-high-risk participants was done in 2019–20. Linkage to hospitalisation records was described as **planned**.
- **Access:** "Researchers in China can apply for data by submitting a research proposal." Applications go through expert review and a data use agreement. A web-based remote platform was said to be in development.
- Trial registration: [NCT02536456](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02536456); pilot protocol — [BMJ Open 2016, PubMed 26729395](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26729395/)
- **Key publications:**
  - Lancet 2017 hypertension (see Q1).
  - Primary-care institutions and hypertension care, Lancet Glob Health 2022 ([link](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/langlo/article/PIIS2214-109X(22)00428-4/fulltext)).
  - Healthy lifestyle and mortality among 0.9 million adults ([PMC8684211](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8684211/)).
  - Healthy lifestyle, statins and mortality in high-risk people ([ScienceDirect 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666667724000035)).
  - LDL-C and mortality heterogeneity (Chin Med J; [link](https://www.ovid.com/jnls/cmj/fulltext/10.1097/cm9.0000000000003199~association-and-its-population-heterogeneities-between)).
  - CKM syndrome stages and mortality (Chin Med J; [link](https://www.ovid.com/jnls/cmj/fulltext/10.1097/cm9.0000000000003914~association-of-cardiovascularkidneymetabolic-syndrome-stages)).
  - Familial hypercholesterolaemia prevalence (Atherosclerosis 2022; [link](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0021915022001605)).
  - Clean-air policy and lipids (J Hazard Mater 2024).
- **ChinaHEART omics (so far small):** a nested case-control study measured 28 MSD-platform proteins in ChinaHEART plasma to predict incident carotid plaque, and tested transportability in UKB (BMJ Open 2026) — local report [87], https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-110220

### Inferences
- ChinaHEART's strengths are scale, national geographic spread (county-level contrasts, policy natural experiments), treatment-gap and care-cascade questions, and cause-specific mortality. Its weaknesses:
  - Lipids and glucose are point-of-care, so there is measurement error.
  - The sample is not population-representative.
  - Deep phenotyping covers only a subsample.
  - Non-fatal events rely on active follow-up of high-risk people, so the ascertainment differs between high-risk and non-high-risk strata. This matters for any incidence model.
- The ~2.7 million stored samples could, in principle, support nested omics or genotyping studies. Any genotyping or export falls under China's Human Genetic Resources regulations (the same constraint CKB notes for raw DNA data).

### Gaps
- I could not verify whether ChinaHEART has **genome-wide genotyping or large-scale proteomics** on its biobank as of 2026. None was found. Only the 28-protein MSD nested study was identified.
- The status of hospitalisation / medical-insurance linkage for non-fatal MI and stroke (and stroke subtype) after 2023 is unverified.
- The "JAMA Netw Open" ChinaHEART papers in the brief were not specifically retrieved. The profile says several exist (e.g., on statin use and on aspirin/antihypertensive use), but I did not verify titles.
- The current enrolment total after 2022, and whether the project continues to recruit, is unverified.

## Q4. Other Chinese/East Asian resources, risk scores and guidelines

### Takeaway
CKB is the main Chinese genomic and proteomic resource: 512k adults, about 100k+ genotyped on a custom Axiom array, and Olink Explore 3072 on about 4,000 people with pQTL maps. About 36% of fine-mapped cis-pQTL credible sets differ from European ones. Japanese (BBJ), Taiwanese (TPMI, ~560k) and Korean (KoGES) biobanks add East Asian GWAS power. China-PAR is the guideline-endorsed Chinese risk equation, while Western equations (PCE) calibrate poorly in Chinese populations.

### Cited Findings
- **CKB design:** 512,726 adults aged 30–79 enrolled 2004–2008 in 10 areas (5 urban, 5 rural). More than 100,000 were genotyped on custom Affymetrix Axiom arrays and imputed to about 21 million variants using 1000G Phase 3. 31.9% had a genotyped second-degree or closer relative (39.0% rural vs 22.8% urban). Death-registry and health-insurance linkage recorded >1.2 million incident disease events through Jan 2019. GWAS summary statistics are public through a PheWeb browser (pheweb.ckbiobank.org). Raw DNA data access is restricted under Chinese regulations — [Walters et al., CKB genotyping, PMC10435379](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10435379/)
- **CKB proteomics and pQTL:**
  - Olink Explore 3072 (2,923 proteins) in 3,974 adults. pQTLs were found for 1,784 proteins, including 1,312 cis-pQTLs for 1,264 proteins. 36% of fine-mapped credible sets differed from European ones. 153 pQTLs for 140 proteins fell at loci with no UKB-PPP association, despite a sample about 10× smaller — [medRxiv 2023.11.13.23298365](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2023.11.13.23298365v2.full) (still shown as a preprint in my searches; journal publication not verified)
  - Olink vs SomaScan comparison in 3,976 Chinese adults, covering 2,168 proteins. Of 1,694 one-to-one matched proteins, 765 Olink and 513 SomaScan proteins had cis-pQTLs, and 400 had colocalising cis-pQTLs (Nat Commun 2025) — [Nat Commun 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-56935-2)
  - Associations of 2,923 Olink proteins with demographic, lifestyle, environmental and health traits (Eur J Epidemiol 2025) — [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10654-025-01311-z)
  - UKB 52,164 people with 2,919 proteins, replicated in CKB 1,937 people: 636 proteins associated with any CVD, 118 replicated in CKB, and MR plus colocalisation supporting causal proteins (Nat Cardiovasc Res 2024) — [Nat Cardiovasc Res 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44161-024-00545-6); local report [12]
  - Plasma proteomics for IHD drug targets in CKB (JACC 2023) — [JACC](https://www.jacc.org/doi/10.1016/j.jacc.2023.09.804); CKB drug-target page — [ckbiobank.org](https://www.ckbiobank.org/achievements/drug-targets)
  - Another Chinese pQTL resource: genome-wide genotype–serum proteome mapping for cross-ancestry cardiometabolic differences (Nat Commun 2023, Chinese cohort) — [Nat Commun 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-36491-3)
- **CKB other CVD work:** early-adulthood BMI shows a monotonic dose–response with CVD, including haemorrhagic stroke (Lancet Public Health 2024) — local report [88]
- **Biobank Japan:** about 270,000 individuals with DNA and clinical data and about 200,000 with serum. Established 2003 at IMS, University of Tokyo, and disease-based (47 diseases). PheWeb at pheweb.jp — [BBJ](https://biobankjp.org/en/); [BBJ PheWeb](https://pheweb.jp/); [BBJ profile, PubMed 28189464](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28189464/)
- **Taiwan:** TPMI has >560,000 participants with genotypes on custom TPMv1/v2 arrays plus EMR (Nature 2025). It is described as the largest genotype+EMR dataset for Han Chinese and reports >388,000 variants not found in other Asian biobanks — [Nature 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09680-x); [TPMI site](https://tpmi.ibms.sinica.edu.tw/)
- **Korea:** KoGES has recruited >210,000 participants, and the custom Korea Biobank Array was launched in 2014 — [KoGES cohort profile, PMC5837648](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5837648/); [Korea Biobank Array, Sci Rep](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-37832-9). A GWAS of 72,298 Koreans across 76 traits found hundreds of new loci — [PMC9903843](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9903843/)
- **China-PAR:** derived in 4 Chinese cohorts (n=21,320) and validated in 14,123 and 70,838. C-statistic 0.794 in men and 0.811 in women, with good calibration (χ² 13.1, P=0.16 and 12.8, P=0.17). Compared against PCE (Circulation 2016) — local report [1], https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.116.022367
- China-PAR is recommended by the 2019 "Guideline on the assessment and management of cardiovascular risk in China" (Joint Task Force) — [Chin Med J China-PAR PCI paper](https://mednexus.org/doi/10.1097/CM9.0000000000003467)
- **Western scores in China:**
  - Calibration of PCE is inconsistent. In CIMIC it overestimated 5-year risk by 67.2% in men and underestimated it by 9.8% in women. In Fangshan it underestimated risk by 76.2% in men and 88.2% in women — [CHERRY study, JACC Asia](https://www.jacc.org/doi/10.1016/j.jacasi.2021.10.007) (search summary)
  - Recalibration of PCE, China-PAR and PREVENT in China — [PMC13153916](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13153916)
  - Meta-analysis of China-PAR predictive power — [PMC11149947](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11149947/)
  - In CKB (n=42,937) and JBPCD (n=14,125), C-statistics were 0.723–0.778 in the general population but only 0.584–0.629 in T2D, with E/O 1.47–3.78 (overestimation) across CKB-CVD, Globorisk, Framingham, WHO 2019 and SCORE2-Diabetes — local report [5], https://doi.org/10.1111/dom.71212
  - China-AIHeart, a Transformer trained on 156,790 people in CKMCC, reached C 0.767 in men and 0.780 in women. That is not better than China-PAR's published 0.794/0.811, but those figures come from different cohorts (EHJ 2026) — local report [7], https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehag517

### Inferences
- **Questions ChinaHEART is especially well placed to answer (inference):**
  1. Care-cascade and treatment-gap epidemiology (hypertension, statins) at county level, including the effect of primary-care capacity.
  2. Geographic and policy natural experiments, e.g., clean-air policy and salt/potassium substitution rollout after SSaSS.
  3. Cause-specific mortality by stroke subtype, where linkage coding allows.
  4. Recalibrating China-PAR, PREVENT or SCORE2-Asia-Pacific at national scale.
  5. Nested omics in the ~2.7 million stored samples, with CKB pQTL maps as genetic instruments and for East Asian replication.
- CKB is the natural external source of genetics and proteomics for ChinaHEART: pQTL instruments, GWAS summary stats via PheWeb, and East Asian LD. BBJ, TPMI and KoGES can add GWAS power for East Asian meta-analysis.

### Gaps
- Whether CKB has since released larger proteomics (e.g., Olink on >10k people, or a case-cohort for CVD) or WGS by 2026 is unverified. ckbiobank.org pages returned 403.
- The publication venue of the CKB pQTL preprint (Nature Genetics or elsewhere) is unverified.
- I did not verify whether the 2024–2025 Chinese primary-prevention guidelines still use China-PAR or have adopted newer tools. I found no source.
- Large Chinese-only CAD/AF/HF GWAS beyond CKB and BBJ contributions (e.g., AF GWAS in BBJ, HF in HERMES East Asian samples) were not retrieved this session.
